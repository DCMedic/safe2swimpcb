Validation and baselines

Time-aware validation metrics are generated from finalized historical records.

Modeling design

The training table uses red-or-worse as the research outcome and environmental features such as wind, gusts, wave height, wave period, swell, tide range and season. Validation is time-aware rather than random so future records are not leaked backward into training folds. A monthly-climatology baseline and a one-day persistence baseline are retained for comparison.

Open data and provenance

The original ALERTBAY/PCBFLAGS archive remains immutable primary evidence, and new Safe2Swim observations are appended automatically. To extend the historical record, Safe2Swim also uses audited NWS Tallahassee Surf Zone Forecast Bay-flag reports only on dates where no primary observation exists. Recovered rows remain visibly labeled and retain their source-product URLs; they never overwrite primary evidence.

Important limitations

PCB flags apply across Panama City Beach, while the tide and location context is centered near the St. Andrews entrance. ERA5 and ERA5-Ocean are gridded reanalysis products, not instruments sitting on the St. Andrews beach. Recovered NWS Bay flags are strong secondary evidence, but are not replacements for original PCBFLAGS messages. Historical Purple reporting is treated separately because the recovered source is less complete for that overlay.

No statistical model on this site should ever be used to override double-red closures or any posted beach flag.

Safety first. Safe2Swim PCB is an informational planning tool, not a swimming clearance. Always obey the flags physically posted at the beach, lifeguards, Beach & Surf Patrol, law enforcement, and National Weather Service warnings.
