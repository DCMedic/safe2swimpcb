What do the Panama City Beach flags mean?

Green

Low hazard. Conditions may be calmer, but caution is still required.

Yellow

Medium hazard. Moderate surf and/or currents can be present.

Single red

High hazard. High surf and/or strong currents can make swimming dangerous.

Double red

The water is closed to the public. Do not enter the Gulf.

Purple

Dangerous marine life is present. Purple can appear with another surf-hazard flag.

Why the posted flag matters more than an app

Beach conditions can change during the day. Safe2Swim PCB records public status changes and shows when its cached flag was last verified, but the flag physically posted at the beach and instructions from Beach & Surf Patrol always take priority.

That distinction also matters for historical analysis: a past yellow or red day describes the public hazard decision for that day, not a guarantee about any future date.
