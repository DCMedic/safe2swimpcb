Okaloosa Beach Safety

Okaloosa County Beach Safety’s primary responsibility is Okaloosa Island. Seasonal lifeguard operations expand through the spring, summer and fall, with limited patrols and response during winter.

Know the island

Okaloosa Island is the barrier-island beach corridor between Fort Walton Beach and Destin, known for emerald water, white sand, the Okaloosa Island Pier and Gulf-front public access.

Okaloosa County parks & beach information ↗
