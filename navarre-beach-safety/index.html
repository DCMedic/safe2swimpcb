Navarre Beach checklist

Read the posted flagThe current flag at the beach is more relevant than an earlier screenshot or forecast.
Swim near lifeguardsAsk about rip-current areas and changing surf before entering the water.
Respect structures and currentsPiers, sandbar gaps and other features can create localized current hazards.
Know how to escapeIf caught in a rip current, conserve energy and move out of the flow rather than fighting directly toward shore.
Safety first: Follow Santa Rosa County flags, lifeguards and closures. Know the Gulf provides planning context, not swimming clearance.
