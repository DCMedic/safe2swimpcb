Local safety guide

Panama City Beach safety.

PCB’s posted beach flag is the controlling public-safety signal for surf conditions. Pair it with the National Weather Service beach forecast, lifeguard instructions and awareness of rip currents before entering the Gulf.

Start with the official PCB flag

Panama City Beach publishes current beach conditions through its official visitor and Beach & Surf Patrol resources. Conditions can change during the day, so check the current posted flag when you arrive, not only a screenshot or forecast from earlier.

Understand the flag and the rip-current forecast separately

Florida warning flags summarize overall surf hazards, while NWS rip-current forecasts evaluate the likelihood of dangerous rip currents. They are related but not interchangeable. A lower-hazard flag does not guarantee that a rip current cannot form.
