Local safety guide

Destin beach safety.

Destin Beach Safety and the Destin Fire Control District publish the controlling local beach warning condition. Use that flag together with the NWS Okaloosa Coastal beach forecast before entering the Gulf.

Check Destin’s official flag

The official local warning flag can change as surf conditions evolve. Check the current Destin Beach Safety status and follow lifeguard instructions at the beach.

Rip-current and surf context

The National Weather Service Mobile/Pensacola office covers Okaloosa Coastal beaches, including Destin. That forecast adds rip-current risk, surf height, winds and other coastal hazards that complement the local warning flag.

Destin-specific considerations

East Pass and structuresCurrents near passes, jetties and structures can differ substantially from open beach conditions. Stay in designated swimming areas.
Changing surfWind and swell can change the beach hazard during the day even when skies remain clear.
Use lifeguarded beachesAsk local beach-safety personnel about current hazards before swimming.
Never fight a rip currentIf caught, conserve energy and move out of the current following the shoreline rather than swimming directly against it.
Safety first: The official Destin flag and instructions from Destin Beach Safety take priority over informational data shown by Know the Gulf.
