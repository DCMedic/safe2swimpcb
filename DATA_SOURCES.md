# Data sources and limitations

- **Current PCB flag:** Visit Panama City Beach, “Current Beach Conditions,” which states conditions are provided by Beach & Surf Patrol. The collector records polling time; it may not equal official issuance time.
- **Flag definitions:** City of Panama City Beach beach-flag page.
- **Primary historical flag archive:** User-supplied ALERTBAY/PCBFLAGS text-message export, parsed into structured records. These original records are preserved and always take precedence over any recovered secondary record for the same date.
- **Recovered historical flag tier:** National Weather Service Tallahassee Surf Zone Forecast (`SRFTAE`) products retrieved from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet NWS text-product archive. Many SRFTAE products explicitly state that the listed area beach flags are based on communication with area beach officials and include a `Bay` flag entry. Know the Gulf promotes an NWS Bay flag only when that calendar date is absent from both the immutable ALERTBAY/PCBFLAGS archive and the automatic observation log. Every recovered observation retains its source product ID, URL, and `record_tier=recovered` provenance.
- **Recovery validation:** Across 613 calendar dates where the NWS Bay-flag record overlaps the primary PCBFLAGS/automatic record, the recovered source agrees with the primary latest daily flag on 98.37% of days, with the peak daily flag on 98.69%, and with at least one reported flag on 99.51%. This strong overlap agreement supports its use as secondary historical evidence, but it does not make the recovered tier equivalent to original PCBFLAGS messages.
- **Purple overlays:** Explicit Purple reports found in recovered NWS products are retained. A recovered NWS product that does not mention Purple is treated as Purple-unknown rather than proof that no Purple flag was posted, because historical Purple-overlay reporting is not demonstrably complete. Purple percentages therefore use only records with reliable Purple reporting.
- **Supplemental NWS surf-zone history:** The SRFTAE archive also supplies historical rip-current risk, surf height, UV category, water temperature, winds, and related beach-zone forecast context. These fields are retained separately from the primary flag evidence for research and cross-checking.
- **Measured local coastal observations:** NOAA/NOS station `PCBF1` / `8729210` at Panama City Beach is retrieved through the National Data Buoy Center historical standard-meteorological archive. Know the Gulf derives daily observed wind, gust, air temperature, water temperature, and pressure fields from these measurements. Historical annual NDBC files are quality-controlled; recent `realtime2` observations have only undergone NDBC gross-error checks until they enter the archive.
- **Measured offshore observations:** NDBC station `42039` is used as the regional offshore measured-wave reference. Know the Gulf derives daily observed significant wave height, dominant/average wave period, wind, gust, pressure, and water-temperature context. This buoy is offshore and is not a direct measurement of breaker height at a Panama City Beach swimmer location.
- **Raw NOAA/NDBC policy:** Know the Gulf does not republish the full timestamp-level NOAA/NDBC archive. The enrichment job processes raw source observations in memory, publishes compact daily derived features and station/source metadata, and leaves the raw measurements with NOAA/NDBC as the authoritative upstream record. This avoids duplicating hundreds of megabytes of federal source data while keeping every derived field reproducible.
- **Tropical-cyclone history:** NOAA National Hurricane Center Atlantic HURDAT2 best-track data are parsed into storm-event, nearby-track-point, and daily proximity tables. Distances are great-circle distances from six-hourly best-track positions to the Know the Gulf Panama City Beach reference point. HURDAT2 is retrospective best-track history, not a forecast; these variables are therefore retained for retrospective association research and are not automatically used as operational prediction features.
- **Pre-2017 official-page hunt:** Know the Gulf periodically checks archived copies of official Visit Panama City Beach current-condition pages for candidate pre-2017 flag evidence. Internet Archive captures are discovery evidence only. Candidate rows are never merged into the historical flag master without independent audit and corroboration.
- **West End PCB historical mirror:** Know the Gulf also indexes explicitly dated historical flag-status posts from the independent local West End PCB site. This source has records reaching into 2010 and says its flag updates are posted when Beach & Surf Patrol sends a status text, but it is not an official government archive. Its records are therefore retained as a `community_mirror_candidate` research tier and are audited against later dates already covered by primary/NWS evidence. They never overwrite or automatically extend the master flag history.
- **Additional pre-2017 targets:** retained City of Panama City Beach / Beach & Surf Patrol operational logs, Bay County emergency-management or beach-safety records, legacy ALERTBAY/888777 message exports, archived official condition widgets, and contemporaneous local reporting used only as corroboration.
- **Weather:** Open-Meteo ERA5 for finalized model training. Live/current UI may use Open-Meteo forecast/current fields. Reanalysis is not the same as an on-beach instrument.
- **Marine:** Open-Meteo ERA5-Ocean for finalized historical wave/swell features. It is relatively coarse and represents regional conditions, not exact breaker height at a specific swimmer location.
- **Tides:** NOAA CO-OPS subordinate station 8729136, New Entrance Channel, St. Andrew Bay. Tide predictions are referenced to MLLW.
- **Research model:** Time-aware logistic regression for red-or-worse association/prediction research. Measured NDBC and HURDAT2 context are preserved in the research table, but HURDAT2 best-track fields are excluded from the operational feature set because they are retrospective. The model is exploratory, is not an official rip-current forecast, and is never a swimming clearance.

## Provenance note after the 2026 brand migration

Historical rows may retain `Safe2Swim` or `Safe2Swim PCB` source labels when that was the collector identity at the time of observation. Those labels are intentionally preserved as historical provenance. New public-facing documentation, site metadata, and future collector labels use **Know the Gulf**.
